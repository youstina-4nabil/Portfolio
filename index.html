01 — About

Apps built for real
user needs.

Youstina Nabil, a Flutter developer most at home in mobile app development — the stage where an idea turns into something people can actually open, tap, and use.

Comfortable across the full pipeline: translating UI designs into clean, responsive Flutter screens, structuring app logic and state, connecting to Firebase and REST APIs, and shipping a working, tested build. Recent work spans a BMI calculator with live calculation logic, a scored and timed quiz app, a flashcard app backed by local SQFlite storage, and a random-outcome game with a smooth, reactive UI.

Every project is version-controlled on Git and GitHub, and treated as production-style work — tested by hand across devices and refined until the UI feels smooth, not just functional.
